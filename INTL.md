## 1. The thing nobody says

The demand exists. What is missing is not leads — it is **three artifacts you do not own yet**:

| You have | You need | Why it is the blocker |
|---|---|---|
| A website that describes 8 services | **One offer with one number** | A stranger cannot repeat your business back to you |
| Six demos marked "not a client" | **One paying reference in the target market** | Overseas buyers discount everything unverified |
| A gmail.com address and a netlify.app domain | **`@kairven.com` and a real domain** | Every email is judged in 2 seconds on this alone |
| A quote form that sends nothing | **A booking link that works** | You sell lead capture; yours is broken |

None of those costs money. All of them cost a weekend. Nothing below works until the last one is fixed, because **you cannot ask a Dubai clinic for $399 while your own contact form is decorative**.

## 2. Which market — decided by arithmetic, not ambition

A solo operator in Kanpur has two hard constraints: **can you be awake when they are**, and **can you get paid without losing 4% and three weeks**.

| Market | Shift vs IST | Their 09:00–17:00 is your | Overlap you can actually sell in | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **UAE / Gulf** | −1:30 | 10:30–19:30 | All of it | ✅ **Start here** |
| **UK** | −4:30 (BST) / −5:30 (GMT) | 14:30–22:30 (winter) | 14:30–19:00 | ✅ **Second** |
| Canada (Toronto) | −9:30 | 18:30–02:30 | 18:30–20:00 | ⚠️ Year two |
| USA (New York) | −9:30 | 18:30–02:30 | 18:30–20:00 | ❌ Not first |
| Australia (Sydney) | +5:30 | 03:30–11:30 | 09:00–11:30 | ⚠️ Painful |
| Germany / Austria | −3:30 to −4:30 | Fine | Fine | ❌ Cold email is illegal without consent |

**Why UAE first, specifically:**

1. **English is the business language.** No translation layer, no localisation cost.
2. **The timezone is free.** Their full working day is your full working day. A UK client means every call lands in your evening; a US client means 19:30 at the earliest.
3. **Dental and clinic spend is high and price-insensitive relative to India** — a single implant case is worth more than your monthly retainer.
4. **WhatsApp is the default business channel.** Your stack already speaks it. In the UK you would be fighting against phone and email habits; in the Gulf you would be using the thing they already use.
5. **Physically close.** A 3-hour flight. The moment you have two UAE case studies, you can be in Dubai for a week and do five in-person meetings — the highest-converting thing a solo founder can do. You cannot do that in London cheaply.

**Why not the US first:** it is 9.5 hours behind, so your selling hours are 18:30–20:00 or 00:30. It is the most competitive market for exactly this service, and it is where buyers most often ask to see US references. It is the reward, not the entry point.

## 3. The offer — one sentence

> **"When a patient calls your clinic and nobody picks up, they get a WhatsApp within 60 seconds with a booking link. Setup in 7 days. $399, then $129/month."**

That is the entire business. Not websites, not CRM, not "AI automation". Three reasons:

- **It is a leak, not an improvement.** "Improve your marketing" is a cost. "You are losing calls you already paid for" is a wound.
- **It is provable in public.** You can call a clinic twice and *know* before you email them.
- **It is priced under the decision threshold.** $399 is a manager's discretionary spend, not a procurement process.

Everything else — website, CRM, weekly report — is an **upsell after the first invoice is paid**. Kairven sells eight things to a buyer who can evaluate one. Cut it to one.

### Pricing, anchored per market

| Market | Setup | Retainer | Anchor you say out loud |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE / Gulf | **AED 1,499** | **AED 449/mo** | "less than one implant case" |
| UK | **£329** | **£99/mo** | "less than half a day of a receptionist" |
| US / Canada | **$399** | **$129/mo** | "less than one new patient" |
| India (reference only) | ₹12,000 | ₹3,000/mo | "less than one filling" |

**Never show the rupee price to an overseas buyer.** The moment they convert it, your price is anchored to Indian labour and it collapses. Quote in their currency, invoice in their currency, and let the exchange rate be your problem, not theirs.

Keep the same *relative* price in every market. This is deliberate: it means you can serve all three without rebuilding the offer, and it stops you from discounting in the market where you are least confident.

## 4. The zero-proof problem, solved without lying

You have no clients. You cannot fix that by writing better copy. Two honest moves:

**A. The paid pilot.** Three slots, per market, clearly labelled:

> "I am taking three clinics in Dubai at AED 999 setup — half my normal price — because I am building my first case studies here. What you give me is permission to use the numbers and the clinic name. After the first three, it is AED 1,499."

This is not a discount, it is a **purchase of proof**, and saying so out loud is what makes it credible. Cap it at three and stick to it, or it becomes your price.

**B. Build the proof before the sale.** Pick one real business in Kanpur — a dentist you know, a family clinic, anyone. Install it free. Measure it for 30 days: calls missed, calls recovered, bookings made. That is your first case study, and a Kanpur number in a Dubai proposal is infinitely better than six "concept demos".

If you cannot get a single free client in Kanpur, that is information: the thing you are selling may not work, and you should find that out before charging a stranger in another country.

## 6. The three emails

**First contact — the only one that matters**

```
Subject: your clinic missed 2 calls yesterday

Hi [Name],

I called [Clinic] twice yesterday — 11:10 and 19:05 — and both went to
a generic voicemail. I also sent the enquiry form at 21:00; it is still
unanswered.

That is usually the most expensive leak in a clinic, because those
callers are already looking for a dentist and simply call the next one.

I fix exactly that: missed call → WhatsApp with your booking link
within 60 seconds, plus reminders before the appointment. Setup in
7 days, AED 1,499, then AED 449/month.

Worth 15 minutes this week?

Sameer
Kairven · Dubai / Kanpur · +91 73989 48772
Reply "no" and I will not contact you again.
```

Rules that make this work: **one specific observed fact**, **no adjectives**, **a price in the first email** (it filters out 80% of wasted calls), **one opt-out line**, and a **real physical address** in the signature if you send to the US.

**LinkedIn / WhatsApp follow-up after a call**

```
[Name] — following up on the audit I sent. If the missed calls are not
a priority this quarter, tell me and I will stop. If they are, I can
show you a 90-second demo of the WhatsApp reply on your own number.
```

**The one follow-up.** Day 4, one line, then stop. Cold sequences of five emails are what get domains blacklisted.

## 7. Getting paid from abroad

| Layer | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Currency | Invoice in the buyer's currency | Shifting FX onto the client reads as small |
| Collection | Wise Business (USD/GBP/AED/EUR receiving accounts) + Stripe or PayPal for cards | You need a local-looking account number, not a wire to India |
| Terms | 50% before start, 50% before go-live | Your site already says this — keep it |
| India side | Export of services is zero-rated under GST with an LUT; keep the FIRC/FIRA certificate for every inward remittance | You need it for GST filings and to prove the export |
| Tax | **Speak to a CA once** before your first international invoice | ₹2,000 of advice now saves a notice later |
| Contracts | One-page SOW, governing law = India, payment in their currency, 30-day exit | `/proposal` generates it |

Do not accept "we'll pay after it works" from an overseas client you have never met. A deposit is the only signal that survives a timezone.

## 8. Legal reality, by market

**This is not legal advice. Read the regulator's page before any bulk send.** But the practical shape is:

| Market | Cold B2B email | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| **UAE** | Unsolicited commercial email is restricted | Use WhatsApp and referral; ask before adding anyone to a list |
| **UK** | Allowed to registered limited companies, with identity + opt-out | Sole traders and partnerships count as individuals → need consent (PECR). ICO fines reach £17.5m |
| **USA** | Allowed (CAN-SPAM) with truthful subject, physical address, opt-out honoured in 10 business days | "B2B is exempt from CAN-SPAM" is a myth — the commercial-email rules apply |
| **Canada** | **Consent required** (CASL), even B2B | Highest-risk market for cold email; prefer referral |
| **Australia** | Allowed to businesses, with unsubscribe | Spam Act 2003 |
| **Germany / Austria** | **Consent required, even B2B** | Do not cold email. Period |
| **India** | DPDP 2023 — consent needed for marketing | Penalties up to ₹50 crore |

**Non-negotiables you implement once:**

1. A suppression list that survives every campaign — unsubscribes must be permanent.
2. Working opt-out in every email, honoured within 2 business days.
3. No purchased lists. No scraped directory blind-sent. You may *look* at a directory to choose who to research, then write to that one person about something you observed.
4. If they are a clinic handling patient data, your service touches health data. Say plainly what you store: **name, phone, appointment time, and whether the call was answered.** Nothing clinical, ever.

That last point is also the best sales line you have: *"I will not store a single clinical detail."*

## 9. What to measure, and when to stop

Track weekly, four numbers only:

| Metric | Target by week 6 | If below |
|---|---|---|
| Audits run | 40 | You are writing, not selling |
| Emails sent | 10/day | You are polishing instead of shipping |
| Calls booked | 3/week | The email's opening fact is not specific enough |
| Paid clients | 1 by week 6 | Change the offer, not the effort |

**Kill criteria, written down in advance:** if after **60 real audits and 60 sends** you have zero calls, the problem is the offer or the market — change one, not both. If you have calls but no closes, the problem is price or trust — add proof, not discounts.

**Honest ceiling:** one person, one offer, three markets. Four retainers at AED 449 plus two setup fees is roughly **₹65–80k/month** by month four. It does not become a company until you can hand delivery to someone else — and delivery, not sales, is what will break first.

## 10. What not to do

- **Do not install Harvey yet.** It is an autonomous cold-email agent on a Claude/Codex subscription, and it multiplies volume. You have no delivery capacity, no case study, and a UK/CASL problem waiting. Use it in month 4, when one extra client costs you nothing in delivery time. (Reasoning preserved in `SUPERCHARGE.md`.)
- **Do not target three markets at once.** Dubai until you have two case studies, then UK.
- **Do not sell to the US because it looks bigger.** The timezone is 9.5 hours against you and the buyer wants US references.
- **Do not claim an overseas presence you do not have.** "Kanpur, working worldwide" is honest and costs you nothing. A fake Dubai address costs you the client the moment they ask to meet.
- **Do not drop the Indian market entirely.** It pays the bills while the export pipeline warms, and a Kanpur case study is the proof you take to Dubai.
